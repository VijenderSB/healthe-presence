    Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

    Patients are no longer just Googling — they're asking ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Is your practice showing up in AI-generated answers? GEO ensures it does.

    Get AI-Ready NowSee How It Works
    Beyond Traditional SEO

    What is Generative Engine Optimization?

    GEO is the practice of optimizing your digital presence so that AI-powered search engines cite your practice when patients ask health-related questions. While SEO gets you ranked on Google's link list, GEO gets you mentioned by name in AI-generated answers.

    AI-First Discovery

    40% of Gen Z uses TikTok & ChatGPT instead of Google. The shift to AI search is already here.

    Zero-Click Future

    AI Overviews answer queries directly — users may never scroll to traditional results. You need to be IN the answer.

    First-Mover Advantage

    Most doctors haven't heard of GEO yet. Early adopters will dominate AI search results in their specialty.

    Key Differences

    GEO vs SEO — What's the Difference?

    SEO isn't dead — but it's no longer enough. GEO complements SEO to cover the new AI-driven search landscape.
